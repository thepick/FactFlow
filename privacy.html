Google sign-in and saved work

Google sign-in supplies your account ID, name, email and profile picture to identify your account and display your signed-in status. Practice progress and settings can be saved in a private app-specific area of your Google Drive. If your teacher configures classroom reporting, practice results and your account name/email are sent to the configured Google Sheets reporting service.

Google access tokens are used in your browser to read and save the app's files through Google's APIs. The app does not request access to all of your Drive. App code is hosted on GitHub Pages with the existing domain provided through Cloudflare. Local settings, drafts or cached work may remain on your device.

Updating the Google connection

The app can ask you to authorize its previous Google configuration once to recover your saved work into the replacement configuration. Recovery uses the same account, checks copied files and leaves the originals unchanged. Saved work is not uploaded to GitHub.

Your choices

You can cancel Google sign-in, sign out, revoke access in your Google account, and remove local or Drive files you no longer need. Revoking access does not automatically delete saved copies. Keep a backup before deleting app data, and contact Michael for questions about classroom records.

Google data is used to provide the app's requested features, not sold or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
